# Metrics

* [Loan Delinquency Rate](./loan_delinquency_rate.md) - Percentage of active loans with overdue payments
* [NPA Ratio](./npa_ratio.md) - Non-performing asset ratio against total loan book
* [Transaction Success Rate](./transaction_success_rate.md) - Percentage of transactions completed successfully
* [KYC Completion Rate](./kyc_completion_rate.md) - Percentage of customers with verified KYC status

## Aurora Electronics (Customer Products)

* [On-Time Delivery Rate](./on_time_delivery_rate.md) - % of delivered orders that arrived on or before the estimated date
* [Return Rate](./return_rate.md) - % of delivered order items returned
* [Refund Turnaround Time](./refund_turnaround_time.md) - Average days from return request to refund completion
* [Stock Availability Rate](./stock_availability_rate.md) - % of active SKUs currently in stock
